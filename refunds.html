Cancellation and Refunds Policy

Last updated: October 2026

In short: because access is granted instantly and the full contents are revealed the moment you're in, we do not offer refunds once access has been granted. The exception is if we fail to deliver — then you get your money back in full.

Why there are no refunds after access

This is a digital product delivered in full, immediately. Once you're in, you have seen your results and your 90-day roadmap — the entire thing. There is no way to return it, so we ask you to be sure before you buy.

Everything we can tell you before you pay is on the pricing page: what the assessment covers, what the results include, and how long you get access. If anything there is unclear, ask us first. We would rather answer a question than take money from someone who turns out to want something else.

When we will refund you in full

  • You were charged more than once for the same purchase. We refund the duplicate without being asked, as soon as we spot it.
  • You paid but never got access, and we cannot fix it.
  • The product is unusable because of a fault on our side that we cannot resolve within a reasonable time.

In each of these cases the refund is for the full amount. We will not argue about it.

Cancellation

There is nothing to cancel. This is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. You will never be charged again, and no payment method is stored for future use.

How to request a refund

Email drfaseelaabdulkareem@gmail.com from the address you used at checkout, describing what happened. We respond within 1 working day. Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method and typically take 5–7 working days to appear, depending on your bank.

Chargebacks

If you think something has gone wrong, please contact us before raising a dispute with your bank. We can almost always resolve it faster ourselves.
