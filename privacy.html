Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what we collect, why, and what we do with it. It covers https://drfaseela-careerfinder.netlify.app and the Non-Clinical Career Finder.

The short version: we keep the minimum needed to give you access and support you. We do not store your assessment answers, we do not sell anything to anyone, and we do not run advertising trackers.

What we collect

DataWhy
Your email addressTo send your access link and to support you
Your name, if providedTo address you properly in emails
Your phone number, if provided at checkoutTo reach you if your email bounces
Payment reference and amountTo match your payment to your access, and for our records
A random device identifierTo enforce the 2-device limit on your link
A one-way hash of your IP addressTo spot links being shared widely. The actual IP address is never stored
Your browser typeTo help diagnose problems if you report one
Whether you finished the assessmentSo we know the product is working

What we do not collect

  • Your assessment answers are not stored. They are used to calculate your results and are then discarded. We cannot see what you answered, and neither can anyone else.
  • Your results are not stored. They exist in your browser while you're looking at them.
  • Your payment details. Card, UPI and bank information goes directly to Razorpay. It never touches our systems.
  • No advertising trackers, no third-party analytics, no cookies for marketing.

Who else is involved

  • Razorpay — processes your payment and tells us your email so we can deliver your access.
  • Netlify — hosts the site and stores your access record.
  • Brevo — sends your access email.

Each only receives what it needs to do its job. We do not sell, rent or trade your information to anybody, for any purpose.

How long we keep it

We keep your access record while your access is valid and for up to 12 months afterwards, so we can help if you come back with a question and for our own accounting records. After that it is deleted.

Your rights

Email drfaseelaabdulkareem@gmail.com and you can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. We'll action deletion requests within 30 days. Note that deleting your record ends your access, since the record is what grants it.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Access links are long random strings that cannot be guessed. We hash IP addresses rather than storing them. Only the site owner can see the buyer list, and it is password-protected.

Children

This product is intended for graduates and working professionals. It is not directed at anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
