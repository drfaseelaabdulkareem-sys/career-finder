Delivery Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This is a digital product. Nothing is physically shipped, so no delivery charges apply and no courier is involved.

How you receive it

Access is delivered by hand, in four steps:

  1. You pay on the Razorpay payment link shown on our website.
  2. You send us the payment screenshot as an Instagram direct message.
  3. We reply with your personal login ID and password.
  4. You type them into the login box on the same page and start straight away.

How long it takes

Usually within a few minutes, and always within 24 hours of us seeing your payment confirmation. We are a one-person business in Kerala, so if you pay late at night your login may arrive the next morning.

If more than 24 hours have passed and you have heard nothing, message us again on Instagram or email drfaseelaabdulkareem@gmail.com. We will either get you your login or refund you in full.

How access works

  • Your login is personal to you and is not transferable.
  • It works on one device. Whichever device you first sign in on becomes your device, so use the phone or laptop you will actually work on. Signing in somewhere else is refused.
  • Access lasts 30 days from your first sign-in, not from the day you paid. You lose nothing by starting later.
  • Keep the message with your login in it. It is how you return to the worksheet.
  • If you change or lose that device, message us and we will move your login to the new one.

If delivery fails

If you've paid and genuinely cannot access the worksheet, contact us. We will either get you in or refund you in full. You will not be left having paid for something you never received.
