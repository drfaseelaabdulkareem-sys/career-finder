Non-Clinical Career Finder

A paid online career assessment for MBBS, BDS, BAMS, BHMS, pharmacy, nursing, physiotherapy and life science graduates who are considering work outside direct patient care.

What it is

A 14-question assessment, taking about 7 minutes, which ranks 12 non-clinical career paths against how you prefer to work. You receive your top 3 role matches, an explanation of why each fits, the parts of each role you would likely find frustrating, a 90-day action roadmap, entry-level job titles to search for, and one experiment to try before committing to a path.

Delivery
